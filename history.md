# History

Corrections and revisions are recorded here. Earlier versions remain in the Git history.

## 11 November 2026: first public version

- Each confirmed site page names the assessing archaeologist, quotes their written reply verbatim (German, with an
  English translation; contact details removed), gives the submission label, the official site number where one
  exists, a 150 m LiDAR hillshade, and a salted SHA-256 commitment to the exact submitted coordinates.
- Locations are given to municipality level. Exact coordinates are withheld.
- P01: the city archaeologist's field photograph (metadata removed) and a redacted Gmail screenshot of his reply
  showing the photo as an attachment.

- 178 locations submitted to 22 authorities; 90 with a written assessment.
- 4 tier-A sites (D01 to D04), 1 tier-B site (P01), 15 tier-C record additions, 8 tier-D outcomes.

### Corrections carried into this version

- **R02 (Hertfordshire HER MHT40508).** Added to the HER on 5 October 2026 as an undated mound. On 11 October our own
  check of every dated Environment Agency survey of the spot found open ground with no mound in January 2003 and a
  1.16 m mound by March 2009. The mound is modern. The record addition stands as the authority made it; we do not
  describe this feature as possibly ancient.
- **R05 (Somerset HER 58699).** Recorded on 6 October 2026 as a probable round barrow, with the discovery credited to
  the submitter. On 22 October the officer said he is now uncertain of an archaeological origin and would consult
  colleagues. It is listed at tier C, not as a confirmed discovery.
