## How it was found

Proposed by both detectors in the pool (S_D + PROX and D1).

The location was screened against the public monument register and reviewed by language-model readers before it was
submitted. The project owner then sent it, with LiDAR views, to the responsible authority.

## Assessment

Written confirmation by the district archaeologist that it is a previously unknown burial mound. No field visit, excavation, date or record number reported.

## The batch it came from

Emsland batch of 15 locations, assessed 29 October 2026: 3 previously unknown (D01, D02, D03), 6 already known (2 of them previously marked in the wrong place in the authority's records), 4 not burial mounds, 2 uncertain.

## Withheld

Coordinates and site-identifying images are withheld to protect the site; see the [README](../../README.md#why-exact-locations-are-not-published).
Researchers can ask the assessing authority.
